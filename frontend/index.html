How a motion travels

Five clicks of the wheel

One motion is on the floor at a time. It is filed with a bond, classified by AI validators, voted on, can be challenged once, then settled by plain code.

📜
01

File

A member files an amendment, grant, admission or expulsion and attaches exactly the bond. The charter, roster and treasury are frozen into the motion.

🧠
02

Classify

Validators answer one yes/no question per article: would this motion touch it? They must match exactly. The tier is computed in code.

🗳️
03

Vote

The tier sets the threshold: majority, two thirds, four fifths or unanimity of the frozen roster. A passing count never ends voting early.

⚖️
04

Challenge

Think the first reading was too lenient? Post a bond. An inclusive re-read can raise the tier, never lower it and pays you a bounty.

🏛️
05

Enact

Anyone can finalize. The effect applies, bonds return, payouts become pull balances. No owner, no admin, no upgrade path.

Why it is built this way

Small rules, strictly enforced

🎯 Exact-equality AI consensus

Booleans, tiers, thresholds: every decision-bearing value must be identical between leader and validators. No tolerance band. Only the free-text reason may differ.

🔒 A one-way ratchet

A challenge can only add touched articles. Entrenchment grows, it never loosens, and weakening an entrenched article needs that article’s own supermajority.

💰 Bonds with teeth

Bonds are escrowed. A successful challenger wins their bond back plus half of the proposer’s. A motion nobody votes on forfeits its bond to the commons.

🧾 Pull payments

Payouts credit a balance; people withdraw() themselves, so a failing recipient can never block the assembly. Bounced transfers return to the treasury.

🧩 No admin, no oracle

One contract file. No owner, no upgrade path, no off-chain authority. Parameters are fixed at deployment and the charter changes only through motions.

📊 A conservation ledger

get_ledger proves the contract balance covers treasury + escrow + claimable at all times. Tested with 190 tests and 49 mutants.
